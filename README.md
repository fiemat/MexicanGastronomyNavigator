I created this application after moving to Mexico and realizing that, in spite of speaking Spanish, I had no idea what I was reading when looking at Mexican food menus. The words are different here, the cuts of meats are different, etc. 
This application is used to help Mexican Food newbies and/or explorers navigate the foods and drinks found on menus across mexico; from street stalls to fancy restaurants
I hope you enjoy this application and find it useful - I welcome your comments or additions
